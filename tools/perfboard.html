Grid 0.1 in (2.54 mm), to scale. Solid wire = top side, dashed = bottom side; a wire is soldered at every hole it bends at. Drag parts and wire points (wire ends on a pin stay attached and follow; right-click or long-press an end to detach). Window: drag left→right to pick what is fully inside, right→left to pick what it touches; drag the picked set to stretch. Auto-size trims the board to the parts plus 2 holes. Pinch or scroll to zoom, drag the board to pan. Edits stay in this browser (localStorage) until you Export JSON and commit it as docs/boards/<board>.layout.json. scripts/check_boards.py runs the same check as this page.
