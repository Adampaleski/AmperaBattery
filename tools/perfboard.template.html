Grid 0.1 in (2.54 mm), to scale. Solid wire = top side, dashed = bottom side; a wire is soldered at every hole it bends at. Drag parts and wire points (wire ends on a pin stay attached and follow; right-click or long-press an end to detach). Window: drag left→right to pick what is fully inside, right→left to pick what it touches; drag the picked set to stretch. Auto-size trims the board to the parts plus 2 holes. A part can't be dropped where its body or pins land on another part or a wire (red ghost, it snaps back); wires already under a body are flagged in the check. Window/crossing also picks board edges and corners: drag them to grow or shrink the board by whole holes. Right-click or long-press a row letter or column number to insert or delete rows/columns. Undo/redo: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y or ↶ ↷. Install shows the build order step by step. Pinch or scroll to zoom, drag the board to pan. Edits stay in this browser (localStorage) until you Export JSON and commit it as docs/boards/<board>.layout.json. scripts/check_boards.py runs the same check as this page.
