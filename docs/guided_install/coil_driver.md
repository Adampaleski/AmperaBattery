## What it is
The board has three low-side N-FET channels (SOURCED, PINMAP S01–S03). Each FET switches one contactor coil's **−** lead to ground:

| Channel | Teensy A pin | Wire | Coil | Coil load |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q-B+ (Main+) | A:11 | S01 | EV200HAANA B+ | 3.8 A max inrush for 130 ms, then 0.13 A hold @12 V (SOURCED, TE datasheet) |
| Q-PRE (precharge) | A:12 | S02 | AEV14012 | 0.35 A @12 V, ~0.41 A @14 V, not economized (SOURCED, Panasonic via Sparky) |
| Q-B− (Main−) | A:21 | S03 | EV200HAANA B− | as Q-B+ |

Coil **+** does not come from this board. F-COIL feeds it (10 A, PROPOSED, PINMAP:177) through the e-stop NC blocks (E01 feed, E02 to both EV200 coils, E03 to the AEV14012 + Kelly KSI).

- **AEV14012 (Q-PRE):** plain 1N4007 directly across the coil terminals at the coil, cathode (band) to +12 V. No zener.

- **EV200HAANA coils (Q-B+ / Q-B−):** **no external diode.** The TE datasheet says the built-in economizer "limits back EMF to 0V" (**SOURCED**; /workspace/teryx/perfboard/BOM.md:36,54 agrees).

## 2. Board layout
**Perfboard (default, Sparky 05:35/05:36).** Three identical channels side by side:
- Gate input → RG (100 Ω) → FET gate.
- RP (10k) from gate to the FET source.
- FET drain → coil-out terminal.
- All three FET sources join one ground bus. That bus leaves on ONE ground terminal back to 12 V − at TB-GND.
- Keep the gate/logic side on one edge and the coil terminals on the opposite edge.

**PCBWay (alternative, only once the circuit is proven, Sparky 05:36).**
- 2-layer, ≤100×100 mm, through-hole parts only.
- Coil traces ≥1 mm at 1 oz copper, with a back-layer ground pour.
- Mounting holes and screw terminals.
- Print the layout 1:1 and check it against the real parts. Run the board house's design check before you pay.
- Cost (typical, not a live quote): ~$20–40 all-in, 1–2 weeks.

## 3. Build — step by step
1. **Board prep** (`cd_board`). Mark three channel columns: B+, PRE, B−. Mark the ground bus along one edge. Leave room for 4 M3 standoffs.
2. **FETs** (`cd_fet`, S01/S02/S03). Fit Q-B+, Q-PRE and Q-B−, flat face up, pins **G-D-S** left to right as printed on the IRLB8721 datasheet. Check the pin order before soldering (**SOURCED** /workspace/teryx/perfboard/BOM.md:33 "G-D-S").
3. **Gate resistors** (`cd_rgate`, S01–S03). Put each 100 Ω in series between that channel's gate-input terminal and the FET **G** pin, close to the pin.
4. **Pull-downs** (`cd_rpull`, S01–S03). Put each 10 kΩ between the FET **G** pin and that FET's **S** pin, on the FET side of the 100 Ω. This keeps the FET off while the Teensy boots (Sparky 05:35; chief review #10).
5. **Ground bus.** Join all three **S** pins with solid-core 18–20 AWG or a solder-filled trace, not thin jumper wire. Run one lead to the board's GND terminal.
6. **Coil outputs** (`cd_term`, S01/S02/S03). Each FET **D** pin goes to its coil-out terminal with solid-core 18–20 AWG:
   - Q-B+ → EV200 B+ coil − (S01, RD/BU coil lead)
   - Q-PRE → AEV14012 coil − (S02, RD/GN)
   - Q-B− → EV200 B− coil − (S03, RD/BK) — coil-lead colors from PINMAP S01–S03 (PROPOSED)
7. **Gate inputs** (`cd_term`, S01–S03). Three gate-input terminals plus one signal-GND terminal from Teensy A:
   - Teensy A:11 → B+ input (S01)
   - A:12 → PRE input (S02)
   - A:21 → B− input (S03)
8. **Flyback diode at the coil** (`cd_flyback`, S02/E03). Fit D-PRE (1N4007) **across the AEV14012 coil terminals, at the relay**, not on this board (**DECIDED**, Sparky 05:41):
   - **Band (cathode) to coil + / +12 V terminal** (E03 feed from e-stop block 2).
   - Anode to the coil − terminal (S02 lead to the Q-PRE drain).
   - A reversed diode shorts F-COIL when Q-PRE turns on.
   - No diode on the EV200 coils.
   - Insulate the leads (heat shrink) and strain-relieve them so vibration can't break the leads at the terminals.
9. **Single-point ground.**
   - Board GND terminal → TB-GND, the single-point GND block (PINMAP TB-GND).
   - Teensy A signal ground already lands at TB-GND (G04).
   - Join the board's signal-GND input there too. Do not run a second ground path.
   - (**SOURCED**, Sparky 05:35: "Join the Teensy's signal ground at that single point.")
10. **Coil + feed** (F-COIL, E01–E03). This is not on the board. Confirm the coil + leads come from the e-stop NC blocks fed by F-COIL. For the bench, use a current-limited 12 V supply in place of F-COIL.
11. **Strain relief and mounting.** Tie every off-board lead down; never let a bare solder joint hold a wire. Mount on standoffs in a sealed box. Conformal coat or hot glue goes on **after** the PASS checks below (Sparky 05:35).

## 4. PASS / FAIL checks (12 V bench only, pack disconnected)
| # | Check | How | PASS | FAIL → fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gate pull-down present (S01–S03) | Unpowered, meter Ω from each gate input terminal to board GND | ≈10.1 kΩ (10k + 100 Ω, DERIVED); PINMAP check "gate-GND ≈10 kΩ" | Open or 0 Ω: RP missing, wrong pad, or a solder bridge |
| 2 | D-PRE orientation (at the AEV14012 coil) | Meter diode mode across D-PRE, coil leads disconnected from the board, red on the anode (coil −), black on the band | ~0.5–0.7 V one way, OL reversed (typical silicon diode, UNVERIFIED for your meter) | Reads 0 V → shorted; reads backwards → diode reversed. Fix before applying 12 V |
| 3 | Fail-safe OFF | 12 V coil supply on, Teensy unpowered or gate inputs unplugged | All 3 coils stay OFF (no click; supply current ≈0 A) | A coil pulls in: pull-down missing or FET D-S shorted |
| 4 | Drive from the Teensy at 3.3 V | Power Teensy A, then drive A:11, A:12, A:21 HIGH one at a time (bench sketch/command per firmware, UNVERIFIED) | Each coil pulls in fully (solid click, stays in) | Chatter or weak pull-in: IRLB8721 RDS(on) is only rated at ≥4.5 V gate. Stop and report to Sparky (Sparky 05:31) |
| 5 | Coil current | Supply readout (or meter in series) per channel at 12 V | AEV14012 ≈0.35 A (≈0.41 A @14 V); EV200 ≈0.13 A hold after the 130 ms inrush (≤3.8 A peak) (SOURCED) | Far off spec: wrong coil, wrong channel, or a bad joint |
| 6 | FET temperature | Finger-touch after 1 min hold, all on | Barely warm (Sparky 05:35, no heatsink) | Hot: FET not fully on (see #4) |
| 7 | Release | Drive each gate LOW | Coil drops out | Stays in: FET shorted. PRE drops slower than the EV200s with a plain diode (expected) |
| 8 | E-stop cut | All three on, push the e-stop | All three coils drop (E02/E03 cut coil +) | Any stays in: e-stop wiring (ESTOP.md) |
